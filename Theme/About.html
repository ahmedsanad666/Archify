About us

We are a collective of architects and designers dedicated to creating spaces that endure, inspire, and elevate the human experience through meticulous craftsmanship.

Crafting shadows and light

Founded on the belief that architecture is as much about the spaces in between as the structures themselves, Studio Noir approaches every project with a rigorous attention to detail and a profound respect for context.

Our process is inherently collaborative and deeply tactile. We draw inspiration from the raw materials of the earth, shaping them to interact playfully with natural light, resulting in environments that feel both grounded and ethereal.

For over three decades, we have remained committed to a philosophy of quiet luxury—design that doesn't shout, but rather reveals its complexity slowly over time through everyday use.
